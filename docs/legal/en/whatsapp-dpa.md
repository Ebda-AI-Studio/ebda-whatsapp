# Ebda Chat Data Processing Agreement

This agreement is between the merchant that uses Ebda Chat (the "Controller") and Ebda AI Studio (the "Processor"). It forms part of the Ebda Chat Terms of Service and applies to personal data that the Processor handles for the Controller in the Service, under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 ("PDPL").

## 1. Subject matter

The Processor receives, stores, routes and sends WhatsApp messages and manages contacts, campaigns, opt-outs and the optional AI agent for the Controller. The data is the Controller's customers' phone numbers, names, messages, media, tags, notes, consent records and order references. The duration is the time the Controller uses the Service, plus the deletion period in section 10.

## 2. Documented instructions

The Processor processes the data only on the Controller's documented instructions, which are these terms, the Terms of Service and the Controller's use of the Service's features and settings. If the Processor believes an instruction breaks the law, it tells the Controller. The Processor does not use the data for its own purposes and does not train AI models on it.

## 3. Controller's responsibilities

The Controller is responsible for a lawful basis for the data, for valid consent to marketing and records of it, for honouring opt-outs and for telling its customers about the processing.

## 4. Confidentiality

Everyone at the Processor who can reach the data is bound by a duty of confidentiality and receives access only as needed for their work.

## 5. Security measures

The Processor applies appropriate technical and organisational measures, including: encryption in transit; encrypted storage of business tokens and of merchants' own model keys, bound to the specific workspace; separation of each merchant's data in its own workspace; role-based access control with owner and staff roles; limits on staff access to production data; audit logs of sensitive actions; no raw phone numbers or message content in billing ledgers; and nightly backups that are checked and expire after 30 days. The Processor reviews these measures as the Service changes.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Controller gives general authorisation for these sub-processors:

| Provider | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare | Hosting, storage and AI inference | Global network; the AI model (Workers AI) runs inside our hosting provider |
| Sentry | Error monitoring | Error reports without message content |
| Kashier | Payment processing for wallet top-ups | Handles card payments for the Controller as payer; receives payment data, not message content |

6.2 Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) is not a sub-processor of the Processor. It is a separate, independent controller for WhatsApp, and delivers and receives messages under its own terms.

6.3 The Processor gives the Controller at least 30 days' prior notice of any new or replaced sub-processor, by email or inside the Service. The Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Controller may close its account and receive a refund according to the Terms of Service.

6.4 The Processor imposes data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than these, and remains responsible for them.

## 7. Assistance with data subject requests

The Processor gives the Controller the tools and help it reasonably needs to answer requests from data subjects, including erasing a contact, exporting data and recording opt-outs. If a data subject contacts the Processor directly, the Processor refers them to the Controller. Deletion requests can also be made through https://ebda.app/en/legal/data-deletion/.

## 8. Personal data breach

The Processor notifies the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, with the information it has: what happened, the data and approximate number of people affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken. It then gives updates as it learns more, and helps the Controller meet its own notification duties.

## 9. International transfers

Data may be processed outside Egypt, because the hosting provider runs a global network and because Meta delivers WhatsApp messages. The Processor transfers data outside Egypt only in line with the requirements of the PDPL, including any approval, licence or safeguard that law requires for a transfer, and with contractual protection from the receiving provider. The Controller agrees to these transfers as described in this agreement.

## 10. Deletion or return

Within 30 days after the end of the Service, the Processor deletes the Controller's data or returns it on request. Backups expire within 30 days. The Processor keeps only what the law requires it to keep, for example financial records, and these do not contain customers' phone numbers or names.

## 11. Audit and information

The Processor gives the Controller the information needed to show compliance with this agreement. The Controller may ask for it once a year, or sooner after a breach, with reasonable notice, and may carry out an audit, or have an independent auditor do so, subject to confidentiality, to security of other merchants' data and to limits that avoid disrupting the Service. Each party bears its own costs unless the audit finds a material breach.

## 12. Liability and law

Each party's liability under this agreement is subject to the limitation of liability in the Terms of Service, and these terms are governed by the same law and courts as the Terms of Service (the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, the courts of Cairo, after 30 days of good-faith negotiation). Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited under mandatory Egyptian law.

## 13. Order and language

If this agreement conflicts with the Terms of Service on the processing of personal data, this agreement prevails on that point. If the Arabic and English versions differ, the Arabic version prevails. Contact: privacy@ebda.app.
