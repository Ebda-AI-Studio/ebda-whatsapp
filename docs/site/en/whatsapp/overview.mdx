# Ebda WhatsApp

Ebda WhatsApp is the WhatsApp product from Ebda AI Studio. It lets a merchant talk to customers on WhatsApp from one place, tied to the store.

## What you get

- **Shared inbox.** Every customer conversation in one screen. Assign a conversation to a team member, add tags and private notes, and search the messages.
- **Link with Ebda Store.** Built in, with no setup on your side. Order confirmations, cash-on-delivery (COD) confirmation, shipping updates and ordering inside the chat all come from your store's data. Ebda Store stays the owner of orders, prices and stock.
- **AI agent.** Answers customers from the knowledge you give it, and hands over to your team when needed.
- **Campaigns.** Send an approved template to a segment of customers who agreed to receive marketing. Customers who opted out, or have no consent on record, are never included.
- **Wallet.** A prepaid balance in EGP, used only when the shared number is your sender (see below).
- **Your own number or the shared number.** Connect your own business number, or start on the shared number.

## Own number or shared number

| | Your own number | Shared number |
|---|---|---|
| What customers see | Your business name and number | The platform's number; the customer picks your store from a list |
| Setup | Connect through Meta's Embedded Signup | Join from the app |
| Marketing campaigns | Yes | No |
| Who pays Meta | **Meta bills your own card directly** | **The wallet**, at Meta's price |

Ebda AI Studio adds no margin to Meta's fees at the moment (0%), and this may change with notice first. The category Meta bills under at delivery is the one that counts.
