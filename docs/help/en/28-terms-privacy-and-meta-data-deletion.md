# Terms, privacy and data deletion from Meta

## The legal pages
They are on the Ebda AI Studio site, in Arabic and English (the Arabic version prevails if they differ):
- **Terms of use:** ebda.app/legal/whatsapp-terms/
- **Privacy policy:** ebda.app/legal/whatsapp-privacy/
- **Data processing agreement:** ebda.app/legal/whatsapp-dpa/
- **Data deletion:** ebda.app/legal/data-deletion/

There is no link to them inside the Ebda Chat app itself today, so save these addresses. Some clauses (such as refunds and VAT) are still under legal review, as the [wallet and refunds guide](09-wallet-top-ups-and-refunds.md) says.

- Deleting your whole store: [delete your workspace guide](12-delete-your-workspace.md).

## When someone removes the app from Facebook and asks for their data to be deleted
Meta sends us a signed deletion request. What happens:
1. We remove the number connections that person made with Ebda Chat.
2. The store owner sees a notice on **WhatsApp number** ([alerts guide](25-alerts-and-notices.md)). Nothing disconnects by itself.
3. Some requests end as "needs review". A person on our team finishes those by hand because they involve a legal decision. There is nothing for you to do.
4. The person can follow their request on a status page with the tracking code Meta gives them.
