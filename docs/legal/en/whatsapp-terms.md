## 6. Prepaid wallet

6.1 The wallet is a prepaid balance in Egyptian pounds (EGP). You top it up by card payment through our payment provider. The card fee is shown and added on top of the amount you enter, and the wallet is credited exactly the amount you entered. The balance is credited when the payment provider confirms the payment.

6.2 Because Meta prices in US dollars, we convert each charge to EGP at the exchange rate in effect when the message is delivered, plus an exchange-rate buffer that covers movements between the rate and the time of payment. The rate, the buffer and the resulting price are stored with each statement entry, and past entries are not recalculated.

6.3 Overdraft: for urgent messages (service replies inside the 24-hour window and verification codes) the wallet may go below zero by up to 50 EGP. Marketing messages stop at zero and never go below zero. A negative balance is a debt, and your next top-up first pays that debt. On the shared number, an urgent message that cannot be paid waits up to 48 hours for a top-up, then fails and we notify you.
