# TPL-EDIT-COUNT-1 - only edits of APPROVED templates count toward Meta's edit limit

Ruling TPL-EDIT-COUNT-1 - the `template_edits` log records an edit only when the template was APPROVED at that moment. Meta limits edits of an APPROVED template to 1 per 24 h and 10 per 30 days and states REJECTED and PAUSED templates are unlimited (`developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-management`). Counting the unlimited edits would block a merchant who just fixed a rejection from touching the approved template for a day. Meta does not say whether edits made while PAUSED count once the template is approved again. Cost if wrong: a merchant may hit Meta's own limit once without our local wait message and sees Meta's text (`meta_rejected`) instead.
