# The weekly summary

A simple picture of your store's last week on WhatsApp, inside Ebda Chat.

## Where to find it
Open **Wallet**. You will find a card called **This week**, with the period above it (from a date to a date). It shows:
- Customers who wrote
- Messages received
- Messages sent
- Messages that failed
- Assistant replies
- Chats handed to your team
- Spent on WhatsApp messages
- Wallet balance

If your wallet or your AI credits are running low you also see a warning: top up so messages do not stop ([wallet guide](09-wallet-top-ups-and-refunds.md)).

## Switch it on or off
Press **Switch the weekly summary off** if you do not want it. The card then says it is off, and you can press **Switch the weekly summary on** any time. The owner and the team can both see it.

## Notes
- It is counts only: no customer name, number or message text.
- It appears inside the app only. It is not sent by e-mail or WhatsApp.
- If you see "We could not load the weekly summary right now", open the page again in a while.
