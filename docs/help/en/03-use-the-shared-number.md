# Use the shared number

The shared number is one WhatsApp number run by the Ebda AI Studio team that you can use without verification or connecting your own. It suits you if you want to start quickly.

## How it works
- Your messages go out from the shared number under your **store name**, so customers know the message comes from your store.
- A customer's reply reaches the store that wrote to them last. If more than one store wrote to the same customer within the last 30 days, the customer gets a short list asking **"which store do you want to continue with?"**, and their message waits until they choose. If nobody wrote to them first, they get one polite note and nothing reaches any store. No store ever sees another store's conversations or customers, and writing to someone else's customer never takes them over: the customer chooses. Each store's name on the shared number must be clearly different from the others'.
- If a customer sends **STOP**, they are opted out of your store (and of every store that wrote to them, if we cannot tell which one they meant).
- It is a **paid add-on** that you switch on from Packages. Its price is set by the Ebda AI Studio team.

## What you need before you start
- An Ebda AI Studio account and an **active (or trial) package that includes the shared number**. If your package does not include it, joining is refused with the reason "your package does not include the shared number": activate a package that does, then try again.
- Wallet balance (see [Wallet](09-wallet-top-ups-and-refunds.md)). On the shared number **the wallet pays Meta's cost**.

## Steps
1. Open **Numbers** and choose **Turn on the shared number**, then confirm the store name customers will see.
2. Accept the add-on terms.
3. Top up the wallet if the balance is zero.
4. Check the templates available for the shared number and wait for Meta's approval if still pending. Inside the 24 hours after a customer writes, you can reply freely.
5. Send a test message to your own phone.

## Cost
- Each message is charged at Meta's price with no markup; **Ebda AI Studio's margin is currently 0%**. This may change later and we will announce it first.
- **Service replies** (a reply within 24 hours of the customer's message) are charged at a steady, predictable price all month. At month end we compare what you were charged with what Meta actually billed for the number, and **any difference in your favor is credited back to your wallet**, shared among stores in proportion to the service replies each used. This does not apply to your own numbers, because Meta bills you for those directly.

## If your package ends
Your store stays on the shared number and **customers' replies keep reaching you**, and you can answer them (free-text replies inside the 24 hours). But you **cannot start new conversations** (template messages) until you have an active package that includes the shared number again. Marketing is already stopped when a package lapses.

## Troubleshooting
- **A message was not sent and shows "insufficient balance":** top up the wallet. Marketing messages stop at zero. Urgent service messages may go down to the overdraft limit (50 EGP), then you must top up.
- **A customer replied and I cannot find it:** they may have written to another store on the same number; we ask them which store before it reaches you. Their message is kept until they choose.
- **I want to stop using it:** choose **Turn off the shared number** in Numbers. Replies stop reaching your store after that.
- **I want my own number instead:** follow [Connect your own number](02-connect-your-own-number.md). You can keep the shared number alongside it or switch it off.
