# Connect your own number to Ebda WhatsApp

You can connect your own WhatsApp number so customers see your business name. The connection happens inside Ebda WhatsApp using Meta's official flow (Embedded Signup).

## What you need before you start
- A Meta Business account where you are an admin, preferably verified ([guide 1](01-verify-your-business-on-meta.md)).
- A phone number that can receive an SMS or call with an activation code.
- **The number must not be active on the WhatsApp app** (regular or Business). If it is, delete the account from the app first. Note: deleting the account removes its old chats from the app, so keep a copy if you need them.
- A suitable display name (see the rules below).
- A valid card or payment method on Meta, because **Meta bills you directly** for the messages that are charged (see [Meta fees explained](10-meta-fees-explained.md)).

## Steps
1. In Ebda WhatsApp open **Numbers** and choose **Connect a new number**.
2. A Meta window opens. Sign in with the Facebook account that manages the business.
3. Choose your Business Portfolio and WhatsApp Business account, or create new ones.
4. Enter the phone number and verify it with the code sent by SMS or call.
5. Enter the display name, a short description and the business category.
6. Add a payment method on Meta if it asks (this is your own account, not Ebda AI Studio's).
7. Accept the permissions and choose **Finish**. You return to Ebda WhatsApp automatically.
8. Wait a moment. The number appears as **Connected**, with its daily limit and quality rating.

**The PIN:** Ebda WhatsApp creates the two-step verification PIN (6 digits) automatically and stores it encrypted. You do not need to enter it. If the number already had an old PIN, turn that off first.

## Display name rules
- It must reflect your real business and be close to the name on your website or papers.
- Not just generic words (like "store" or "company"), and not the name of a famous person or someone else's brand.
- No extra punctuation, emoji or the word "WhatsApp".
- Meta reviews it and may reject it or ask for a change. That is normal.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Number is already registered on WhatsApp":** delete the account from the WhatsApp app on your phone, wait a little, and try again.
- **Display name rejected:** change it to something closer to your real business name and resubmit from WhatsApp settings in Meta.
- **The code does not arrive:** try "call" instead of "text", and check that the number can receive.
- **The window closed before I finished:** start again. Ebda WhatsApp does not charge for the connection itself.
- **The number shows "Disconnected":** open Numbers and choose **Refresh status**. If it persists, disconnect the number and connect it again.
