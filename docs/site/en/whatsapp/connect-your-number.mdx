# Connect your number

You connect your own number from inside Ebda WhatsApp, using Meta's official flow (Embedded Signup). Only the store owner can do it.

## What you need

- A Meta Business account where you are an admin, preferably verified. See [Verify your business on Meta](https://help.ebda.app/en/whatsapp/verify-your-business-on-meta/).
- A payment method on your Meta account, because Meta bills you directly for the messages it charges for.
- A number that is either in the WhatsApp Business app (recommended) or not active on any WhatsApp app.

## The two paths

- **Keep the WhatsApp Business app on the same number (coexistence).** The number stays in the app on your phone and Ebda WhatsApp works next to it. Both see the same conversations. Messages typed in the app show in Ebda WhatsApp marked as sent from the app.
- **Move the number to Ebda WhatsApp only.** The number leaves the app. Best for a brand-new number.

## Steps

1. Open **Numbers** and choose **Connect a new number**.
2. Complete Meta's window: sign in, choose your business and WhatsApp Business account, enter the number and the display name, then **Finish**.
3. You return to Ebda WhatsApp automatically. The number shows as **Connected** with its daily limit and quality rating.

Finish the Meta window and return quickly: the one-time code Meta gives us expires after 30 seconds. If the setup stops halfway, the number shows as not finished and you can retry from **Numbers** without repeating Meta's window.

For a number kept on the Business app, the chat history and contacts transfer can run only once, within 24 hours of connecting. Imported contacts come without marketing consent.

The full detail, display name rules and troubleshooting are in the help guide: [Connect your own number](https://help.ebda.app/en/whatsapp/connect-your-own-number/).

## Doing it from code

Embedded Signup needs a browser window, so the connection itself is a merchant action. The endpoints behind it are listed in the [HTTP API](/whatsapp/api).
