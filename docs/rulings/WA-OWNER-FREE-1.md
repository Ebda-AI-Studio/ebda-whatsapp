# Ruling WA-OWNER-FREE-1

**Decision:** (1) The assistant's free turns are also capped per OWNER: `OWNER_FREE_TURNS_PER_DAY` (= the per-workspace allowance, 150) per rolling day across every workspace of the Store owner (`workspace_owners`), taken with one conditional D1 INSERT into `assistant_owner_turns` (migration 0035; never cached, so it is NEVER-stale). A workspace with no owner row is its own owner (`ws:<id>`): never locked out, never unmetered. The per-workspace counter stays. (2) A voice transcription that the speech model completed is charged to the merchant's AI credits at the credit the code already holds for a turn (`AI_TURN_CREDITS` = 1, the same hold that was being released) instead of being released when the turn ends silent or as a hand-over; a call that threw (slot given back, VOICE-SLOT-1), a declined note and BYOK stay free. The platform's real speech cost per note is still recorded separately (`recordTurnCost`).

**Evidence:** `src/core/smart-layer/quota.ts` (`takeOwnerTurn`), `src/core/ai/agent.ts` (`speechRan`), `test/assistant-owner-free-turns.test.ts`, `test/voice-note.test.ts` (old code: balance unchanged after a silent or hand-over voice note; no refusal at the owner's 151st turn).

**Cost if wrong:** the 150-per-owner number and the one-credit price for a transcription were chosen from what the code already holds, not a priced decision: a legitimate multi-workspace owner may hit the shared cap earlier than they expect (they see `free_cap`), and a one-credit charge for a note that cost the platform less (or more) than a credit is rounded in the merchant's favour or against it until Ahmad sets a price.
