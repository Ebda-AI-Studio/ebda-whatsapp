# Meta fees, explained simply

Meta charges for messages by **message type**, not by word count. Prices differ by the country you send to and change from time to time.

## Message categories
- **Marketing:** offers and campaigns. The most expensive.
- **Utility:** order confirmation, shipping, COD confirmation.
- **Authentication:** verification codes.
- **Service:** your reply to a customer's message within the 24-hour window. Meta treats these specially (part of them may be free); check Meta's page for current details.

## Who pays Meta?
| Your case | Who pays | How you see the cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Your own number** | **Meta bills your own card directly** | We show Meta's cost for information, without deducting from the wallet |
| **The shared number** | **The wallet**, at Meta's price | Every entry in the statement |

Ebda AI Studio adds no margin to Meta's fees right now (**0%**). This may change, with notice first.

## How it is calculated
- The cost is counted when the message is **delivered** and Meta considers it billable, not when you send it.
- If the message fails or Meta does not charge for it, nothing is deducted.
- The category Meta charges under is the one that counts, even if it differs from what we expected.
- Meta's prices are in US dollars, so they are converted to pounds at a set exchange rate.

## To keep costs down
- Reply quickly inside the 24-hour window (service replies cost less).
- Send marketing only to interested customers.
- Pick the right template category.

## Troubleshooting
- **I was charged for a message that did not arrive:** open the statement, check the entry, and send us the message ID.
- **The cost differs from the estimate:** the estimate is approximate; the actual cost follows the category at delivery.

Current prices and the country list: see the WhatsApp Business pricing page on Meta's website.
