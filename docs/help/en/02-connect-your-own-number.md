# Connect your own number to Ebda Chat

You can connect your own WhatsApp number so customers see your business name. The connection happens inside Ebda Chat using Meta's official flow (Embedded Signup).

There are two ways, and Meta shows you the choice during the flow:

- **Keep using the WhatsApp Business app on the same number (recommended).** Your number stays in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, and Ebda Chat works next to it. You answer from the app or from Ebda Chat, and both see the same conversations. You do not lose anything.
- **Move the number to Ebda Chat only.** The number is removed from the app and runs only through Ebda Chat. Use this for a brand-new number.

## What you need before you start
- A Meta Business account where you are an admin, preferably verified ([guide 1](01-verify-your-business-on-meta.md)).
- For the recommended path: the **WhatsApp Business app** (not the regular app) installed on your phone with the number active, on a recent version.
- For the second path: a number that can receive an SMS or call with an activation code, and that is **not active on any WhatsApp app**. If it is, delete the account from the app first. Deleting removes its old chats from the app, so keep a copy if you need them.
- A suitable display name (see the rules below).
- A valid card or payment method on Meta, because **Meta bills you directly** for the messages that are charged (see [Meta fees explained](10-meta-fees-explained.md)).

## Steps
1. In Ebda Chat open **Numbers** and choose **Connect a new number**.
2. A Meta window opens. Sign in with the Facebook account that manages the business.
3. Choose your Business Portfolio and WhatsApp Business account, or create new ones.
4. Enter the phone number. For a number on the Business app, Meta asks you to confirm from the app on your phone; otherwise verify it with the code sent by SMS or call.
5. Enter the display name, a short description and the business category.
6. Add a payment method on Meta if it asks (this is your own account, not Ebda AI Studio's).
7. Accept the permissions and choose **Finish**. You return to Ebda Chat automatically. **Do this within a few seconds of the last Meta screen**: the connection code Meta gives us expires after 30 seconds.
8. Wait a moment. The number appears as **Connected**, with its daily limit and quality rating.

## If WhatsApp flags your number
WhatsApp watches the quality of every number (how many customers block or report you). If it flags your number or rates it poor, **Numbers** shows a badge and a short explanation, Ebda Chat **pauses your running campaigns** to protect the number, and we also stop sending **new template messages** from it until WhatsApp says it recovered; you can still reply to customers inside the 24-hour window. When the number recovers, **campaigns do not restart by themselves**: check your messages and your audience, then resume the campaign yourself.

## Your old chats and contacts (Business app numbers)
When Meta asks, you can share your chat history (up to 180 days) and your contacts with Ebda Chat. This transfer can run **only once, and only within 24 hours of connecting**. If you decline, or miss the 24 hours, you would have to disconnect and connect again to get it. Your contacts come in **without marketing permission**: you can only send them marketing messages after they agree. Old messages are shown in the chats but never open the 24-hour window and never trigger automatic replies.

Messages you type in the WhatsApp Business app appear in Ebda Chat marked as sent from the app. A number kept on the app sends at most 20 messages per second.

## The PIN (new numbers)
Ebda Chat creates the two-step verification PIN (6 digits) automatically and stores it encrypted. You do not need to enter it. If the number already had an old PIN, turn that off first. If registration fails 10 times, Meta **locks the number for 72 hours**; wait for the lock to pass, then choose **Try again** on the number.

## Display name rules
- It must reflect your real business and be close to the name on your website or papers.
- Not just generic words (like "store" or "company"), and not the name of a famous person or someone else's brand.
- No extra punctuation, emoji or the word "WhatsApp".
- Meta reviews it and may reject it or ask for a change. That is normal.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Number is already registered on WhatsApp":** if you want to keep the Business app, restart and pick the Business app option. Otherwise delete the account from the WhatsApp app on your phone, wait a little, and try again.
- **"The number is locked for 72 hours":** there were too many registration attempts. Wait for the lock to end and try again.
- **The setup stopped halfway (the number shows "Not finished"):** open Numbers and choose **Try again**. You do not need to repeat the Meta window.
- **Display name rejected:** change it to something closer to your real business name and resubmit from WhatsApp settings in Meta.
- **The code does not arrive:** try "call" instead of "text", and check that the number can receive.
- **The window closed before I finished, or "the code expired":** start again. Ebda Chat does not charge for the connection itself.
- **The number shows "Disconnected":** open Numbers and choose **Refresh status**. If it persists, disconnect the number and connect it again.
