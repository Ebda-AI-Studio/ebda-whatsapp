# Sign in, change the language and sign out

You open Ebda Chat with the same Ebda AI Studio account you use for Ebda Store. There is no separate password for Ebda Chat, and the page itself never asks you for one.

## Who can sign in
The store owner and any team member who has been given a seat. The difference between the roles is in the [team and seats guide](14-team-members-and-seats.md). If you sign in and find nowhere to work, you have no seat yet: the owner needs to give you one.

## Sign in
1. Open wa.ebda.app.
2. Press **Sign in**.
3. Use your Ebda AI Studio account (the same one as Ebda Store).
4. You land in the **Conversations** of your store.

## Change the language
At the top of the conversation list there is a language button, labelled with the other language (**English** or **العربية**). Press it and the whole screen switches; the direction flips between right-to-left and left-to-right. Arabic is the default. Phone numbers and links always stay left-to-right, even inside Arabic text.

## Sign out
Press **Sign out** at the bottom of the side rail. What the app keeps in your browser is cleared. On a device that is not yours, also sign out of your account at the sign-in provider so the next person cannot get in with it (the page reminds you).

## If you cannot get in
- **"We could not sign you in"** with a reason underneath: the sign-in provider refused or cancelled the request, the request was already used, or the answer did not match the request. In every case press **Try signing in again** and start from the beginning; do not reopen an old sign-in link.
- **"We could not reach the sign-in service right now":** the service is not answering. Wait a moment and press **Try again**.
- **You were signed out suddenly and saving says "session revoked":** your session was closed. Sign in again.
- **The page says sign-in is not configured:** open Ebda Chat from your own portal, or ask whoever set the app up for you.
- On a phone the side rail becomes a navigation menu, and the orders panel opens as a sheet from below.

If it still does not work, send ahlan@ebda.app the message you see on screen.
