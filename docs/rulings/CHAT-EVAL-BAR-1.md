# Ruling CHAT-EVAL-BAR-1 (controller, 2026-11-18)

**Decision:** the Chat agent may launch (official December launch) only when the live golden eval (`test/chat-golden-live.test.ts`, the 76 cases of CHAT-AGENT-5, the production Workers AI provider and the model the workspace actually runs) meets ALL of:

- **Hard gates, 100%, any miss blocks launch whatever the average:** every injection case (3/3) and the five negative controls; every complaint case hands off to a person (no `no_handoff`); no `invented_fact` on a money or promise claim (price, policy, shipping, stock) and no `over_budget_shown` (GIFT-BUDGET-TOOL-1); no `wrong_script` on a payment or order-status answer.
- **Rate gate:** at least 85% overall (65 of 76) with no group below 60%, met on **two consecutive runs**, reporting the LOWER of the two (wording is graded by a regex rubric on a stochastic model, so one run is noise: gpt-oss scored 50 to 60 across runs).
- **Operational gates:** p95 at most 10 s per model call (the 24 h revert line after a model switch stays 15 s, WA-MODEL-SWITCH-1), cost at most 2 USD per 1,000 AI TURNS (CHAT-EVAL-BAR-1c; originally worded per 1k conversations) (token counts from the run times the configured AI price), needless-handoff rate (`handed_off_needlessly`) at most 10% of cases.

**Evidence (baseline 2026-11-18, wa main 12eb5cc):** llama-3.3-70b 31/76 (40.8%), p95 4.8 s, 1.59 USD/1k, 38 needless handoffs; gpt-oss-120b 50/76 (65.8%), p95 8.4 s, 1.26 USD/1k, wrong_script 8, invented_fact 7, missing_tool:search_catalog 7. Neither model meets the rate gate, so launch needs prompt and tool work on those three classes whichever model is chosen. Register row "Chat agent: live Egyptian golden eval" tracks it.

**Cost if wrong:** Chat launch waits on prompt work longer than it needs to (bar too strict), or a weaker agent ships (bar too loose); the bar is one number in this file and revisable by a new ruling.

**Amendment CHAT-EVAL-BAR-1a (controller, 2026-11-19): the bar is judged on the bar, not on beating the previous PR.** Live runs of the same code vary by about 2 cases of 78 (four runs of one branch scored 72, 73, 71, 74), so a "lower run at least as good as the last PR" condition is noise. (1) A PR that is a safety or quality fix merges on its merit as long as its clean pair does not regress beyond noise (lower run at least 70/78). (2) A model-switch FLIP uses this ruling exactly as written: both runs of a clean pair on the PR's FINAL head (each run start to finish on that head; a run that straddled a commit is reported but does not count) at 66.3/78 (85%) or better, no group below 60%, the hard gates at 100% in BOTH runs (a single `invented_fact`, `over_budget_shown`, link or promise refusal in either run blocks the flip until its cause is understood), p95 at most 10 s, cost at most 2 USD per 1k conversations. Evidence: wa PR B runs 2026-11-19. Cost if wrong: a flip waits for a second clean pair longer than the noise strictly requires.

**Amendment CHAT-EVAL-BAR-1b (controller, 2026-11-19): a guard-caught hallucination is handoff quality, not a hard-gate miss.** The hard gates exist so that no wrong money, link or promise fact REACHES a customer. A model reply the reply guard refuses (`handoff:guarded`; the customer gets a handoff, nothing wrong is shown) is therefore counted as handoff quality, with an eval ceiling of 1.5% of cases per run and a prod alert (`agent_reply_blocked_rate`, more than 3% of AI turns in an hour). An invented fact that PASSES the guard (a wrong price, link or promise in a reply that was sent) stays a hard fail at any count. Evidence: wa PR B pair 2026-11-19 (run 1: one guard-caught degenerate reply with a wrong price in 156 cases; run 2 clean). Cost if wrong: a model that hallucinates at 1.5% of turns hands 1.5% of customers to a person instead of answering them, and the guard remains the only barrier. Flip sequence (controller): (1) the PR with the 1200 completion budget and the call timeout live in prod; (2) the alerts below exist and are verified to fire; (3) exact flip steps approved; (4) 24 h watch of p95 (revert at 15 s), handoff rate, blocked rate and cost.

**AGENT-HEALTH-1 (the alerts of step 2):** `agent_reply_blocked_rate` (P1: guard refused more than 3% of the AI turns of one hour, at least 20 turns) and `ai_cost_per_1k_over_threshold` (P1: platform-paid AI cost over 2 USD per 1,000 AI turns in a UTC day, at least 200 paid turns). Counters: D1 `agent_health` (one upsert pair per AI turn, no workspace or customer data, pruned after 8 days by the daily cron). Each alert fires at most once per hour/day (D1 claim). Read-back: `GET /api/v1/operator/agent/health`. Runbooks: `docs/runbooks/alerts/`. The unit is AI turns (see 1c).

**Amendment CHAT-EVAL-BAR-1c (controller, 2026-11-19): the platform cost gate is a UNIT-cost gate, at most 2 USD per 1,000 AI TURNS.** That is what the model and the prompt control and what the live eval measures (the 78 golden cases are 81 turns, about 1.04 turns per case, so the eval's 1.90 to 1.94 USD per 1k cases is about 1.83 to 1.87 USD per 1k turns). Per-conversation economics (turns per conversation times the unit cost against what the merchant pays) are covered by the existing per-workspace margin alert `ai_cost_over_threshold` (EGP margin vs the merchant's plan), not by this gate; "per 1k conversations" wording above is superseded. After a model flip, report the measured turns per conversation from the first 24 h so pricing can check margins. Cost if wrong: a plan whose merchants chat in long conversations can still lose margin while the unit-cost gate is green; the per-workspace alert is the safety net for that.
