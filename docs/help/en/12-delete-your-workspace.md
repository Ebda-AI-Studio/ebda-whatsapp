# Delete your Ebda WhatsApp workspace

The store owner can ask us to delete the workspace and everything in it. Support can also do it at your request.

## What happens
1. You confirm the request. Nothing is deleted yet: there is a **14-day waiting period** and you can cancel any time before it ends.
2. After 14 days we stop all sending, disconnect your numbers and delete your conversations, contacts, media, uploaded files, campaigns, AI settings and backups.
3. Records of payments and refunds are kept as the law requires, **without your store's name**.

## Before you ask
- If your wallet still has a balance, either request your refund first ([Wallet](09-wallet-top-ups-and-refunds.md)) or agree in the request that the balance is given up.
- A refund or payment still being processed delays the deletion until it finishes.
- Deleting does not cancel anything at Meta; remove your number from your Meta account yourself if you no longer want it there.

## Who can do what
Only the store owner can ask or cancel. Staff cannot.
