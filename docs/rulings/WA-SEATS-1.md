# Ruling WA-SEATS-1

**Decision:** staff work in Ebda WhatsApp only while the store owner has given them a seat, inside the package's member limit (deny by default). Seats are explicit assignments (`seats` table, DO migration v39); membership is the Store's and is read live over the service binding on every person request, never cached; a Store that cannot answer is a 503, never "allowed". The effective role is the lower of the token's and the Store's, so a stale token never grants more than the Store says now. A downgrade deletes nothing: the earliest assignments keep working up to the new limit, the rest are `over_limit` until the owner frees a seat or upgrades, and one INFO alert (`seats_over_limit`) is raised. Seat rows of people who left the Store are dropped whenever the owner lists the team or assigns a seat, so a leaver never holds capacity. Without a Store link there is no membership source and seats are not applied.

**Evidence:** plan `docs/plans/2026-10-26-packages-limits-seats-yearly.md` task P1b; identity Ruling ID-19 (contract v1.4); the Store side shipped in Store #291 (`WhatsAppBridge.memberRole` / `listStoreMembers`, which answer with the bare value and throw FORBIDDEN / INVALID_ID, not the commerce envelope). Master finding M-061. `test/seats.test.ts`, `test/store-bridge.test.ts`, `web/src/TeamScreen.test.tsx`.

**Cost if wrong:** (a) the per-request `memberRole` call makes every person request depend on the Store worker; an outage is a 503 for staff and owner alike (chosen on purpose: permissions are never-stale); (b) a white-label or unlinked deploy with a missing binding in production would skip seat checking, which nothing new here would catch (the binding is declared in `wrangler.jsonc`; the production deploy check is unchanged); (c) a staff member the Store suspends loses their seat row and needs a new assignment when reactivated.
