# Your team and seats in Ebda Chat

Your package says how many people can work in Ebda Chat, **you included**: "Chat 300 AI replies" and Store-included 2, "Chat 1,500 AI replies" 5, "Chat 5,000 AI replies" 15. You decide who gets the seats.

## How it works
1. Add the person as staff on your store (in Ebda Store). Adding them there does not give them access to WhatsApp yet.
2. Open **Team** in Ebda Chat (store owner only) and press **Give a seat** next to their name.
3. They can now open the inbox. Without a seat they see a page that tells them to ask you.

You always have your place and never need a seat. The seat count on the Team page, "2 of 5 seats", counts you.

## When the seats run out
If the package has no seat left, **Give a seat** answers that the package allows N people. Remove another person's seat, or upgrade your package from the Wallet, and try again.

## Removing someone
- **Remove seat** stops their access to WhatsApp at once, and frees the seat. They stay on your store.
- If you remove a person from your store, or suspend them, their WhatsApp access stops at once as well and their seat is freed the next time you open the Team page. If you bring them back, give them a seat again.

## If you move to a smaller package (or the plan ends)
Nothing is deleted. The people who were given seats first keep working, up to the new limit. The others are paused (the Team page says so) until you remove a seat or upgrade. When your plan is not in effect, only you can work, as for every other feature.

## Good to know
- Only the store owner sees the Team page and can give or remove seats. Staff cannot.
- Names only: we never show your team's emails or phone numbers on this page.
- If we cannot reach your store's team list for a moment, the page says so and changes nothing. Try again shortly.
