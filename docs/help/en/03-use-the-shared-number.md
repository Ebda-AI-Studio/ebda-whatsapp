# Use the shared number

The shared number is one WhatsApp number run by the Ebda AI Studio team that you can use without verification or connecting your own. It suits you if you want to start quickly.

## How it works
- Your messages go out from the shared number and carry the phrase **"on behalf of your store"**, so customers know the message comes from your store.
- Customer replies reach your store in the inbox. If a customer talks to more than one store on the same number, we ask **"which store do you mean?"** so the reply goes to the right place. No store ever sees another store's conversations.
- It is a **paid add-on** that you switch on from Packages. Its price is set by the Ebda AI Studio team.

## What you need before you start
- An Ebda AI Studio account and an active Ebda WhatsApp package.
- Wallet balance (see [Wallet](09-wallet-top-ups-and-refunds.md)). On the shared number **the wallet pays Meta's cost**.

## Steps
1. Open **Numbers** and choose **Turn on the shared number**.
2. Accept the add-on terms.
3. Top up the wallet if the balance is zero.
4. Check the ready-made templates (they include "on behalf of") and wait for Meta's approval if still pending.
5. Send a test message to your own phone.

## Cost
- Each message is charged at Meta's price with no markup; **Ebda AI Studio's margin is currently 0%**. This may change later and we will announce it first.
- **Service replies** (a reply within 24 hours of the customer's message) are charged at a steady, predictable price all month. At month end we compare what you were charged with what Meta actually billed for the number, and **any difference in your favor is credited back to your wallet**, shared among stores in proportion to the service replies each used. This does not apply to your own numbers, because Meta bills you for those directly.

## Troubleshooting
- **A message was not sent and shows "insufficient balance":** top up the wallet. Marketing messages stop at zero. Urgent service messages may go down to the overdraft limit (50 EGP), then you must top up.
- **A customer replied and I cannot find it:** they may have written to another store on the same number; we ask them which store before it reaches you.
- **I want my own number instead:** follow [Connect your own number](02-connect-your-own-number.md). You can keep the shared number alongside it or switch it off.
