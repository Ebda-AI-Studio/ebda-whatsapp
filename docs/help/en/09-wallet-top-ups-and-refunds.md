# Wallet, top-ups and refunds

The wallet is a prepaid balance in Egyptian pounds. It pays Meta's cost on the **shared number**, and the cost of AI credits depending on your package.

## What you need before you start
- An Ebda AI Studio account (the owner tops up).
- A bank card for online payment (through Kashier).

## Top up
1. Open **Wallet** and choose **Top up**.
2. Enter the amount.
3. You will see the card fee **added on top of your amount**, and the wallet is credited **exactly the amount you entered**.
4. Complete the payment. The balance is added when Kashier confirms the payment, not just when you return to the page. If it is late, wait a few minutes and refresh.

*Note:* if the wallet is in debt, your first top-up pays the debt and the rest is added to the balance.

## Overdraft
- There is an overdraft limit of **50 EGP** for urgent messages (service replies inside the window, and verification codes).
- **Marketing messages stop at zero** and never go below zero.
- On the shared number, transactional messages (like an order confirmation) that cannot be paid wait up to 48 hours to be sent after a top-up; after that they fail with "insufficient balance" and we notify you.

## Statement
From **Wallet > Statement** choose a month to see every entry (top-up, message, credits, refund) with totals by category, and download it as CSV. Figures are shown as recorded at the time of the entry.

## Refunds
*(Subject to the final terms of service.)*
- The wallet balance is refundable for **12 months** after cancellation, after any debt is settled.
- Refunds go to the original payment method through Kashier; otherwise by bank transfer or InstaPay after an identity check.
- **The minimum refund is 50 EGP.**
- **Not refundable:** card fees already paid, AI credits that came with the package, the current package period (it keeps working until the period ends), and purchased AI credits that were used or are older than 14 days.
- Financial records are kept even after you cancel or ask for data deletion, as the law requires.

To request one, contact the Ebda AI Studio team through support, or use the refund request in your account if available.

## Troubleshooting
- **I paid but the balance did not grow:** wait a few minutes. If it persists, send the transaction number to support.
- **A campaign stopped with "insufficient balance":** top up and resume the campaign.
- **I was charged for a message that did not arrive:** we only charge messages Meta counts as billable.
