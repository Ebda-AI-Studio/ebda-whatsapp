# Verify your business on Meta

Business Verification proves to Meta that your company is real. Without it your number works, but with tight limits.

## Why it matters
- **Sending limits:** before verification, Meta lets you start conversations with only a small number of customers per day (usually 250 new customers a day to begin with). After verification the limit rises in steps (tiers), depending on the quality of your messages and how customers react. Meta decides the numbers and tiers, and changes them from time to time.
- **Display name:** verification helps Meta accept your business name as the name customers see.
- **Trust:** a verified number is less likely to be restricted.

## What you need before you start
- A valid commercial register (سجل تجاري), as a clear photo or PDF.
- A tax card (بطاقة ضريبية), as a clear photo or PDF.
- A Meta Business account (Business Portfolio) where you are an admin.
- Your website or business page, showing the business name as written on your papers.
- A phone or email that can receive the confirmation code.

## Steps
1. Open **Business settings** in Meta Business Suite and go to **Security Center**.
2. Choose **Start verification**.
3. Enter the **legal name** of the company **exactly** as written on the commercial register, plus its address and phone number.
4. Choose the document type and upload the commercial register, and the tax card if Meta asks for it.
5. Choose how to confirm ownership (email on your company domain, or a call/text to the company phone).
6. Enter the code you receive and submit.
7. Wait for the result. It can take from a day to a few business days. You will see the status on the same page.

## The most important point: everything must match
- Legal name in Meta = name on the commercial register = name on the tax card, letter for letter.
- The address you enter = the address on your documents.
- If your name or address changed, update your papers first, then apply.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Request rejected" or "documents not sufficient":** usually a name or address mismatch, or an unclear image. Photograph the document again in good light and reapply.
- **I do not receive the confirmation code:** try another method (email on the company domain instead of a call).
- **Still "under review" for a long time:** wait, and if it takes very long contact Meta support from the same page.
- **Need help:** the Ebda AI Studio team can guide you through the steps, but the final decision is always Meta's.
