# InstaPay orders in WhatsApp

A customer can order from your catalog on WhatsApp and pay with InstaPay. The order stays **awaiting payment** until your store confirms the money arrived.

## What you need before you start
- Your store on Ebda Store with the **InstaPay** payment method turned on and your InstaPay address (IPA) entered correctly.
- The catalog synced with Ebda Chat.
- A connected number, and **ordering in chat** switched on in the store-link settings.

## How it works (customer side)
1. The customer picks products from the catalog on WhatsApp and sends the cart.
2. They choose **InstaPay** as the payment method.
3. They type their address or pick a saved one.
4. They receive a message with the **InstaPay address (IPA)**, the **amount** and an order **reference**.
5. They transfer from their bank app and send a **photo of the receipt** in the same chat.

## How it works (your side)
1. The receipt is forwarded to your store once, together with the order.
2. **The order stays "awaiting payment"**; it is not confirmed automatically from the picture.
3. You check the receipt in Ebda Store and confirm the money arrived.
4. When the order is confirmed, the customer is told on WhatsApp (if still inside the 24-hour window).

## Notes
- The customer has **48 hours** to send the receipt after receiving the payment details.
- The amount and reference come from Ebda Store; do not change them by hand.
- A second picture sent later is not forwarded again for the same order.

## Troubleshooting
- **The customer sent the receipt and nothing happened:** check that the picture arrived in the same conversation and that less than 48 hours passed.
- **An item ran out of stock:** the customer gets a message naming the item, and it is removed from the cart.
- **131047:** the window closed (over 24 hours). Send the customer a payment reminder template.
- **Money arrived but the order is still pending:** confirm the payment in Ebda Store.
